About me

I'm Nur Alia Safia, an IT (Multimedia Technology) undergraduate at Universiti Malaysia Sabah (UMS).

My studies cover programming, web development, database management and 3D modelling. My final year project, Mysteries of Bidasari, is a cel-shaded 3D game based on Sabah's Kadazan-Dusun folklore. I lead the project's story and level design, UI design, 2D and 3D asset creation, and development coordination, while also contributing to gameplay programming (C#, Unity) and quality assurance through game testing.

Beyond my final year project, I've worked on various academic projects involving programming, augmented reality (AR), 3D animation, web development, UI design and IoT. I've also had the opportunity to present two of these projects to visitors at university exhibitions.

I'm looking for an internship where I can apply what I've learned, build practical skills and gain exposure to a professional IT environment.
